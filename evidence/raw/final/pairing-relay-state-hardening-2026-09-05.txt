DEFECTS CLOSED
- Chrome's unauthenticated pair-response catch-up correctly used only the six
  fixed bootstrap relays, but final pair-complete catch-up incorrectly used the
  same restricted list. Android could therefore become active when only a
  custom relay accepted completion while Chrome remained waiting.
- A paired capture could silently fall back to defaults when its durable relay
  list was absent. A corrupt empty item list also made the computed write
  requirement zero.
- An unused transport probe labeled NIP-11 plus WebSocket connectivity as relay
  health even though it did not prove matching OK or read-back.

CONTROLS
- Before Android is authenticated, Chrome reads responses only from fixed
  defaults. After the exact response/digest is authenticated, completion is
  read from the entire bound set, including custom relays.
- Paired channel state requires protocol v2, a canonical channel key, all six
  ordered defaults, a canonical zero-to-two custom suffix, and a matching
  retained relay-set digest. Compatible earlier-v2 state gets one digest
  backfill from its already authenticated canonical list.
- Pairing recovery recomputes the request digest before network use. Invalid
  request or pairing-key state is cancelled and stripped of its one-time key.
- Channel and item relay sets are validated and compared before quorum math or
  network send. An incomplete channel leaves a new capture unbound and queued;
  an inconsistent already-bound item becomes a retained local failure.
- The unused connectivity-only probe was removed; the dated public smoke
  harness remains the write, matching-OK, exact-read-back evidence source.
